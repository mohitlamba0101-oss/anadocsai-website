Optimise, then fix

Fix what costs the most first, with the formula written for you

The Optimisation report scores every finding on what it really costs at your model's scale, so the list starts with the changes that pay back. Tick the ones you want, send them to Fix Studio, and get fixes you can paste.

Priorities from real impact, not rule counts

A rule broken on a line item nobody reads is not the same as one that slows every recalculation. Each finding is weighed on what it costs here.

  • Size cost from what Anaplan reports or the export measures, never guessed from a grid.
  • Calculation cost, measured when you add Anaplan's Line Items export, estimated from the formula when you don't.
  • Who reads it: how many items depend on the finding and how heavy those readers are.
  • Rules broken, with the Classic and Polaris performance and size checklists alongside.
  • High, Medium and Low sections with filters, and an AI review of the ranking on the cheapest capable model.

Fix Studio: your priority sets the depth

Send findings from the report, or from any line item: every line item opens its formula page with a Findings-and-fix card. Fix Studio keeps the queue, shows each finding's impact and where it is, and asks the right model for the right depth.

  • High: the in-depth fix on Claude Opus, with the analysis, a paste-ready formula, the expected impact, alternatives and a test.
  • Medium and Low: a lighter fix on Claude Sonnet, quicker and cheaper.
  • Collected into a remediation pack you can hand to the builder or attach to a release.
Formulas, explained and written

Read any formula in plain English. Describe a new one and get it built.

Each line item gets its formula, what it does in words, where every number comes from and the rules it breaks. New model owners stop reverse-engineering and start reading.

Classic → Polaris

A senior-consultant migration assessment, generated from your model

Anaplan's direction is Polaris, and there is no converter. AnaDocs AI scans every line item against the documented engine differences and builds the plan around what it finds.

  • Will not work: functions Polaris lacks, summary-method combinations it rejects, over-length formulas, invalid LOOKUP and time-scale patterns.
  • Reconcile: valid formulas whose answers change between engines, pre-flagged for the reconciliation team.
  • Redesign: patterns Polaris amplifies, ranked against your model's own calculation-effort hotspots.
  • The plan: entitlement, port or rebuild, remediation, security, parallel run, cutover and hypercare, with effort and timeline you can tune.

From the built-in demo model. Effort, cost and timeline recalculate from your own assumptions: team size, day rate, contingency, port or clean rebuild.

Security by design

It reads your model. It never changes it, and it keeps no copy.

Model designs are confidential. AnaDocs AI documents structure, not your planning numbers, processes it in memory, and keeps what is personal in your browser.

Never writes to Anaplan

The connector reads structure and metadata, the Monitor reads status, and the Model Planner hands a build pack to your builder. AnaDocs changes nothing in your models.

No stored credentials

The live connection relays your own Anaplan sign-in, read-only and only for the models your account is approved for. Your Anaplan password is never stored, and the Monitor holds no credentials.

Processed in memory

To build the documentation, the model's structure (names, formulas, dimensions, formats, sizes and notes, never cell values) is sent over an encrypted connection and processed in memory. It is kept for at most 30 minutes after your last request, never written to disk or logged, and discarded when you sign out.

Personal data stays in your browser

Model history exports, transcripts, Monitor readings and remembered tabs live in your browser. Before AI sees model history, licence data or workshop transcripts, people's names and e-mail addresses are removed or replaced by tokens in your browser, and only the text the task needs is sent.

Locked down by policy

A strict content-security policy blocks every outbound connection except to the AnaDocs server and the AI provider. AI requests go through our server within your account's limits, and only token counts and cost are kept. Every account is approved by a person before it can connect to Anaplan.

Metadata, not data

Documentation needs structure, not your planning numbers. The connector pulls structure and metadata. The only values it brings in are the ones you ask for, such as a Model History export or list members for Source mapping, and those stay in your browser.

AI output is generated and should be checked against the model before you rely on it. AI runs only when you ask for it, apart from the one short AI review of the Optimisation report's priorities, which runs once per model when you open the report or Fix Studio. AI runs on Accodian's Claude account within the allowance set for your account: our server builds each prompt from your model's structure, your request and the results of the look-ups the AI makes, and sends it to Anthropic. Those look-ups can include data you loaded into the page, such as before and after values from a Model History export or rejected lines from an import-error file; cell values from the model itself are not sent. People named in model history, licence data and transcripts are replaced or removed in your browser first. We store only counts and cost, never your questions, the answers or your model, and a conversation is held only in server memory, until 30 minutes after your last question. Your account record holds your email, name and plan, the name, id and size counts of the models you document, and the token counts and cost of each AI request.
